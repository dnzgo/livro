What is Livro?

Livro is a diary for people who never kept one. You write about your day in your own words — Livro draws it as a comic page, with a character that looks like you.

Most diaries end up as a wall of text nobody reads again — not even the person who wrote it. Livro keeps the part that matters (writing honestly about your day) and turns it into something you'll actually want to flip through.

One entry a day becomes one comic page. Each month's pages are gathered into a chapter on your shelf, and each year becomes a book you can read from the first page or download as a PDF.

The name comes from the Portuguese word for book: over time, your days add up to one.

How it works

Four steps from your day to your book.

  1. Create your character

    You're the main character of every comic. Pick your hair, eyes, face, build, clothes and accessories in a few simple steps — and change them whenever you like.

  2. Write about your day

    Write naturally, like you'd tell a friend. No prompts to follow, no format to get right. Pick black-and-white or colour, then you're done.

  3. Get your comic page

    Livro draws your day as a manga-style comic page. You can close the app while it works — we'll send a notification when it's ready.

  4. Watch your book grow

    Every page joins that month's chapter. Your calendar fills in, your shelf grows, and each year becomes a book.

Features

Small daily habit. Something worth keeping.

Two comic styles

Classic black-and-white manga, or full colour. Choose per entry — some days just feel like ink.

Memory calendar

Twelve weeks at a glance. Yellow days are memories you've kept, green is today. Missed yesterday? You have until noon to write it.

Chapters & books

Pages are collected into monthly chapters and yearly books on your shelf. Read them like a real comic, page by page.

PDF downloads

Download any chapter or a whole year as a PDF with your name on the cover. Save it, print it, or AirDrop it to someone who was there.

English, Türkçe & Deutsch

The whole app speaks all three languages. Switch any time from your profile — every screen changes at once.

Private by design

Sign in with Apple or Google — no new password. Your diary isn't used to train AI models, and deleting your account deletes your memories.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to be good at writing?

Not at all. Write the way you'd text a friend — a few sentences is enough. Livro works out the scenes, the people and the mood.

How often can I write?

Once a day — one entry becomes one comic page. Before noon you can still write about yesterday, in case the day got away from you.

Will the comic match exactly what happened?

Livro uses AI to draw your day, so it's an interpretation, not a photograph. It may picture a place or a moment differently from how you remember it — that's part of the charm.

Is my diary used to train AI?

No. Livro doesn't train its own models on your diary and doesn't opt your content into third-party training. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is sent where, and why.

Can I keep my comics if I leave?

Yes. Premium members can download any chapter or a whole year as a PDF. We recommend downloading the pages you love before deleting your account, because deletion is permanent.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Profile → Premium (or Settings → Subscription), which opens Apple's subscription page. You can also cancel from your Apple account settings. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

Which devices are supported?

Livro is an iPhone app, available through the Apple App Store, for anyone aged 16 and older.
