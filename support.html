Common questions

Quick fixes.

My comic page wasn't created. What now?

Your diary entry is always saved, even if drawing fails. Open that day in the app and tap Try Again. Each day has a limited number of attempts, and the app shows how long you can still try. Trying again is part of Premium. If it keeps failing, contact us with the date of the entry.

I can't sign in.

Check your internet connection and try again. Use the same method you used the first time — Apple or Google — because your diary and comics belong to that account. If it still doesn't work, contact us and tell us which method you use.

I paid for Premium, but the app doesn't show it.

Go to Profile → Premium and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with. This also works after moving to a new iPhone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Profile → Premium (or Settings → Subscription), which opens Apple's subscription page. You can also cancel from your Apple account settings. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple, so Apple handles refunds, not Livro. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I don't get notifications.

In the app, check that notifications are on under Profile → Notifications. Then open your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Livro and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

How do I download my comics?

Premium members can download any single comic page, or a whole month as a PDF chapter from its chapter card. A month's chapter can be downloaded once the month is over, because until then you can still add pages to it.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your diary, your comics and your character, and it can't be undone, so download anything you want to keep first. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription; cancel it in your Apple account settings.

How can I get a copy of my data?

Email us and we'll send you a copy of the personal data we hold about you, including your diary text. The Privacy Policy explains what we store and why.
