Livro Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Barış Görkem Yaşar, the operator of Livro ("Livro", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the Livro application.

Livro is currently available through the Apple App Store and is intended for users aged 16 and older.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller/service provider for Livro is:

Barış Görkem Yaşar
Türkiye

Email: hellolivro@gmail.com

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact:

hellolivro@gmail.com

2. What information does Livro collect?

The information we collect depends on how you use Livro.

Account information

When you create an account, we may receive information necessary to authenticate and maintain your account through:

  • Apple Sign In
  • Google Sign In

Depending on the authentication provider and the options you select, this may include information such as:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • authentication/provider identifier;
  • Livro account identifier.

Livro does not require you to provide a password directly to Livro when using supported third-party authentication.

Your account name is used to greet you in the app and is printed on the covers of your chapter and book PDFs. You can change it in your profile at any time.

Character profile

When you create your character, we store the appearance you choose, such as gender, age range, skin tone, hair, eyes, face shape, body build, height, clothing and accessories. Livro uses it to draw you in your comics and sends it to OpenAI together with your diary text when a comic is created.

You decide how your character looks and can change it at any time. Your character does not need to resemble you.

Device and notification information

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push notification token, its time zone and the date of the last reminder we sent. Notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service. If you turn notifications off in Livro or sign out, the token is deleted from our servers.

We also store your account's time zone, which determines the date a diary entry belongs to.

3. Diary and Memory Content

Livro allows you to create diary entries and memories.

This may include:

  • diary text;
  • dates associated with memories;
  • titles or other information you provide;
  • memory relationships;
  • content generated from your memories;
  • information necessary to organize your memories.

Your diary entries may contain highly personal information.

For example, you may voluntarily write about:

  • relationships;
  • family;
  • emotional experiences;
  • health;
  • beliefs;
  • political opinions;
  • personal events;
  • other private matters.

You should therefore treat Livro as a service that may process highly personal information.

We do not require you to provide information of a particular sensitive category, but the content you voluntarily enter may contain such information.

4. Information Livro Does Not Currently Collect

Livro currently does not provide diary entries through:

  • photo uploads;
  • audio recordings.

Livro also does not currently use a separate analytics platform for behavioral analytics.

However, technical information required for the operation, security, and reliability of the application may still be processed by Livro or its infrastructure providers.

We will update this Privacy Policy if these practices change.

6. Artificial Intelligence and OpenAI

Livro uses the OpenAI API for certain AI-powered features.

When you request an AI feature, relevant information may be transmitted to OpenAI so that the requested operation can be performed.

For example:

Livro does not send all of your stored information to OpenAI simply because you have an account.

Information is sent when it is necessary for an AI-powered feature you request or where technically necessary to provide the relevant functionality.

7. AI Training

Livro does not use your private diary or memory content to train its own AI models.

Livro also does not intend to opt user content into third-party model-training programs.

For OpenAI specifically, Livro uses the OpenAI API rather than consumer ChatGPT services. OpenAI's API documentation states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train OpenAI models by default unless the customer explicitly opts into data sharing.

However, OpenAI may process API data for purposes such as abuse monitoring and service security according to its applicable policies and the configuration available to Livro.

Livro has not opted in to sharing its API data with OpenAI for model training. OpenAI may keep API inputs and outputs for a limited period for these purposes, as described in its data-retention policies.

8. Where your data is stored

Livro uses several infrastructure providers.

Supabase

Livro uses Supabase for:

  • PostgreSQL database infrastructure;
  • persistent storage;
  • application data;
  • diary and memory records;
  • files generated or stored by the Service.

The Livro database is currently configured in Frankfurt, Germany.

Chapter and book PDFs you download are also stored there, so they can be downloaded again quickly. They are deleted together with your account.

Fly.io

Livro uses Fly.io to host its backend application infrastructure.

Livro's backend may temporarily process information, including diary content, when handling requests.

Livro's backend currently runs in Frankfurt, Germany.

Apple

Apple provides:

  • App Store distribution;
  • Apple authentication;
  • subscription and payment infrastructure;
  • delivery of push notifications (Apple Push Notification service).

Google

Google provides authentication services when you choose Google Sign-In.

OpenAI

OpenAI provides AI processing used by certain Livro features.

The privacy practices of these providers are also governed by their respective agreements and privacy policies.

9. International Data Transfers

Livro is operated from Türkiye and is intended to serve users in Türkiye, the EU/EEA, and the United States.

As a result, personal data may be processed in different countries depending on:

  • infrastructure configuration;
  • service providers;
  • authentication;
  • AI processing;
  • technical operations.

Some providers may process data outside the country where you live.

Where applicable law requires specific safeguards for international transfers, Livro will use legally recognized transfer mechanisms or other appropriate safeguards.

10. How long we keep your information

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide Livro and for other legitimate or legally required purposes.

If you delete your account, Livro will initiate deletion of the associated account and User Content.

Account deletion is intended to be permanent.

However, deletion may not immediately remove every technical copy because information may temporarily remain in:

  • backups;
  • security logs;
  • disaster-recovery systems;
  • systems subject to legally required retention.

Such information will be deleted or rendered inaccessible according to the applicable retention and deletion procedures.

Backups are kept for 7 days, so data you delete is removed from backups within 7 days.

11. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Livro account.

When the deletion process is completed, we intend to delete:

  • your account;
  • your diary entries;
  • your memories;
  • your character profile;
  • notification tokens for your devices;
  • generated content associated with your account;
  • stored files associated with your account;
  • other personal data associated with your account,

subject to information that must or may legally be retained.

If you signed in with Apple and stop using Sign in with Apple for Livro in your Apple Account settings, Apple notifies us and your Livro account is deleted in the same way.

Because deletion is permanent, you should export any memories you want to keep before deleting your account.

12. Data Export

Livro provides a PDF export feature that allows you to download supported memories and diary information.

The export is intended to give you a readable copy of your memories.

The PDF export may not include every technical record maintained by Livro, such as:

  • internal database identifiers;
  • system metadata;
  • embeddings;
  • security logs;
  • internal AI-processing records.
